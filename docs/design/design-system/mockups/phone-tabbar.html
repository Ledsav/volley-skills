A floating glass tab bar

Today the bar is a full-width glass strip, and the current page is shown only by darker text. Each option below floats as a capsule above the home indicator, lets content scroll under it, and marks the current page with a shape, not just a colour. Content is the U17 team list, so you can see the glass over colour.

A · Capsule with a selection pill recommended

Five tabs, icon and label, as today. A soft pill sits behind the current tab and slides to the next one with a spring when you switch. Closest to iOS 26 and the least change for coaches who already know the bar.

B · Icons, with the current tab as an ink capsule

Only the current tab shows its label, inside a filled ink capsule. It's the most obvious, but the other tabs lose their labels, which is harder for coaches who open the app once a week.

C · Capsule plus a separate search button

Like A, with search pulled out into its own round glass button (iOS 26's Music and Photos pattern). Exercises moves to More to make room. Search is one tap from every page, but it's a bigger change.

Details that apply to all three

Placement14 px from the sides and 24 px above the bottom edge (plus the safe area), radius = half its height. Page content gets bottom padding so the last row can scroll clear of the bar.
MaterialThe design-system glass recipe (blur 24, saturate 180%) with a hairline light edge on top and a soft floating shadow. With Reduce transparency it turns into a solid surface capsule and keeps its shape.
SelectionThe pill (A, C) or ink capsule (B) is chrome, so it's monochrome, never blue. The current tab also gets aria-current="page" and a semibold label. Press feedback: scale .97 on touch-down.
MotionThe pill slides with the segmented-control spring (response .3, no bounce). With reduced motion it just moves. The bar never hides while you scroll (that would hide navigation from slow readers), and it stays out of full-screen scouting, awards and the diagram editor as it does today.
