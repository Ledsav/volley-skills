What to look for

Is it too grey?

Buttons and selection are black or white, never blue. Colour only appears on levels, grades, live and errors.

Does dark feel equal?

True black background, raised greys for surfaces. Neither theme should feel like the "real" one.

Scouting at a glance

Score, corrections and Undo on top; court in the middle; grades and points at the thumb. Recording taps don't animate.

One main action

Top-right on every screen: "New match", "+" on the phone. Search sits just left of it.
