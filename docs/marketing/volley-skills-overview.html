Overview

The whole season in one place

Coaches plan, train, test and play in separate tools today: a spreadsheet per player, drills in a notebook, stats on paper. Volley Skills joins it up, so every training, test and match feeds what you know about each player.

2

Teams & players

Know every player, at a glance

Each team has a roster in lineup order. Each player has a card that holds everything a coach needs, and nothing ends up lost in a spreadsheet tab again.

A player card: average skill score, level, focus areas, open objectives and the latest physical test.

  • Nine skills scored 1–10, from serve and reception to jump, speed and game IQ, each with a coach note and a priority flag.
  • One level, one colour. The average maps to a level that reads the same everywhere in the app:
    BeginnerDevelopingAdvancedElite
  • Shared scoring guides describe what a 3 or a 7 means for each skill, so every coach in the club scores the same way.
  • Rosters in lineup order (setter, outside, opposite, middle, libero) with search, sorting and starters marked.
  • Contacts and guardians in one place, with consent recorded per player.
  • Match history per player: attack, serve and reception efficiency from every scouted match.
  • Bring your roster in with a bulk import instead of retyping it.

  • Short-term objectives with a target date, a status (active, in progress, attention, completed) and a coach comment.
  • Season objectives for the bigger picture, for each player and for the team as a whole.

4

Exercises, diagrams & trainings

Build better trainings, faster

The club keeps one shared library of exercises. Draw each drill on a court, combine drills into trainings, and put them on a team's calendar.

The court diagram builder: players, balls, cones, ladders, lines, arrows and text.

  • Trainings from ordered exercises, with durations and a running clock, each with its own ID (TR-0001).
  • A calendar per team. Assign trainings to dates; the home page shows the next seven days.
  • Share as a PDF from a phone: the session plan, then a page per exercise with its diagram. Add notes for the players expected that day.
  • Draft with AI (optional): describe the session you want and review the drafted exercises before anything is saved.
5

Match scouting

Scout live. Decide in the moment.

One person can scout a whole match from the bench, one-handed on a phone, and see who is struggling while there is still time to act.

Live scouting on a phone.

  • Tap the court. Usually two taps per action: the player, then the grade. Rotations and the score follow on their own.
  • Live stats during the match: side-out and break-point percentages, reception quality, and alerts when a player keeps making errors or the opponent goes on a run.
  • Fix mistakes in seconds with undo, an editable rally-by-rally timeline and "insert a missed rally here".
  • Or just type it. Describe the rallies in plain words and AI turns them into the same events. Player names are swapped for shirt numbers before anything leaves the device.
  • Bad gym Wi-Fi is fine. Every action is saved on the device first and syncs when the connection comes back.

After the match: a report per player and per fundamental, rotations included, ready to export.

6

Stats & awards

See the season. Celebrate it.

Every scouted match adds up into team statistics. At the end of a match or a season, turn them into awards the players will want to share.

The team Stats tab: record, side-out, break point, attack efficiency and the trend over the season.

  • Filter by competition, date range and venue, and compare rotations, leaderboards and per-player tables.
  • Export the numbers as PDF or CSV.
  • Season and match awards such as top scorer, best server and MVP, picked from the stats with the coach's final say.
  • Present them full screen as a story with a reveal for each award, then share the cards as images or a video.
  • Photos never leave the device. Background removal for player cut-outs runs on the device itself.
7

Built for clubs

Safe for minors. Simple for staff.

Youth clubs hold data about children and their parents. Volley Skills is designed around that from the first line of code.

Access per team and section

Coaches only see the teams they work with. Library, trainings and guides each have their own editors. New people request access and an admin approves them.

No passwords to leak

Sign in with a one-time email link or a Google account.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics or third-party tracking scripts, ever. A plain-language privacy policy is public.

Data rights built in

Consent per player, data export for a player or a whole team, and deletion when someone leaves.

AI only if you want it

AI features are optional and use your club's own provider. Names are replaced by shirt numbers before sending.

Made for the gym

Works on phones, tablets and computers, in light or dark mode, and can be added to the home screen like an app.
