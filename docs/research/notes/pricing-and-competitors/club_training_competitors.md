## 1. Volleyball-specific drill / practice-planning tools

### Takeaway
The volleyball-specific market is split between cheap content libraries (GMS+, AOC, Volleyball1on1: roughly $100-$500/yr per coach or small group) and single-coach mobile apps (free to ~$30/mo). None found combines a club-wide editable drill library with a custom court-diagram builder, player skill profiles, physical testing and per-team calendars with role-based club access; the closest is Italy's VolleyBallSystem (claims season planning + training archive + match analysis) and the app CoAtch Volleyball (player ratings + drill-library practice builder + AI planning), neither with public pricing.

### Inferences
- Volleyball content subscriptions anchor price expectations low (~EUR 60-120 per coach per year; a 10-coach club on GMS+ Small Groups ~ $890 ~ EUR 800/yr). A club-wide tool for Serie B+ must justify a price above this by being the club's system of record (player data, calendars, access control), not a content library.
- The US content leaders (GMS, AOC) sell pedagogy, not software; their practice plans are fixed/printable. A custom court-diagram builder + club-owned library + PDF export is a genuine gap versus them.
- Mobile single-coach apps (CoAtch, iPB, SoloCoach) cover practice building but not club multi-team administration, role-based access, or GDPR-style consent for minors.

### Gaps
- Not found: "Volleyball Coach Clipboard" as a distinct VB product, "Volley Planner", "Drillbook" VB apps, VolleyballXpert, a product called "Volleyball Toolbox" (search mapped it to GMS+), Coach Paint / SportsTG diagram tools pricing.
- No Polish, Spanish or German volleyball-specific training planners surfaced; only Italy's VolleyBallSystem. Prices for VBS and CoAtch not found.
- No volleyball-specific diagram builder with a published price was confirmed (GMS+ shows diagrams but no evidence of a user-editable builder).

- For NCAA and pro clubs, the enterprise budget line for performance software is already in the EUR 10k-30k/yr range (Teamworks, Catapult), but it buys monitoring/ops, not volleyball skill development or drill planning. Our app is complementary rather than a replacement there; a price of a few thousand EUR per club-season would sit well below these.
- XPS is the most direct multi-sport competitor for the "drill library + diagram + session planning + athlete data" bundle in European pro sport, but it is generic (no VB skill rubric) and quote-only.
- Sportlyzer's EUR ~1000/club/yr is a useful European mid-market anchor for club-wide licensing.

### Gaps
- No pricing found for Kitman Labs, Teamworks AMS per athlete, CoachMePlus, Metrifit, Coachbase, XPS, VolleyBallSystem.
- No NCAA volleyball-specific procurement records found; the Teamworks POs are athletics-department wide.
- Catapult volleyball-specific product pricing (e.g., jump/load for indoor) not found beyond aggregate ACV and UK university contracts that bundle hardware.

## 3. Team communication / management substitutes

### Takeaway
Communication/club-admin tools are free or cheap (Spond free, monetising via payment fees; TeamSnap ~$70-130/team/yr; clubs from ~$599/yr) and cover calendars/attendance only, so they set a near-zero price expectation for the "calendar" part of our app but not for development/planning.

### Cited Findings
- Spond app and Spond Club are free; revenue comes from a transaction fee on payment requests via Stripe, varying by jurisdiction, and a percentage on fundraising — [Spond help](https://help.spond.com/en/articles/25998-spond-is-free-to-download-and-use)
- Reported Spond fee rates: UK 2.5% + GBP 0.20 (from competitor SportMember's comparison); US $1.00 + 3.29%, Canada $0.50 + 5% (FitGap) — [SportMember](https://www.sportmember.co.uk/en/clubmanagement/sportmember-vs-spond), [FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/024550/spond)

### Inferences

### Gaps

## 4. Scouting + development + drill library + training planning in one tool?

### Inferences

### Gaps
