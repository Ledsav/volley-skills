# Market size and budgets: semi-pro and pro volleyball clubs (Italian Serie B level and up), Europe and the Americas, 2025-26 / 2026-27

Research date: 2026-10-20. Most counts are for the 2025-26 season (now finished). 2026-27 changes are flagged where found. Where a figure is not sourced, it appears only under Inferences or Gaps and is labeled as an estimate.

## Italy: how many teams play at each tier (Serie B and up)

### Takeaway
Italy alone has about **390 senior teams at Serie B level or above** in 2025-26: about 159 men's and 229 women's. A new women's Serie A3 (16 teams) starts in 2026-27. Serie B (men's B, women's B1 and B2) makes up about 80% of that count. It is a national-level, mostly amateur or semi-pro tier, and several top clubs field second teams in it.

### Cited Findings
- Men, 2025-26: Lega Pallavolo Serie A accepted all 47 applications. That gives **12 SuperLega, 14 Serie A2 and 21 Serie A3** teams. — [Lega Volley: "accolte tutte le 47 domande di iscrizione"](https://www.legavolley.it/2025/serie-a-credem-banca-accolte-tutte-le-47-domande-di-iscrizione/)
- Serie A3 2025-26 had two groups: Girone Bianco (11 teams) and Girone Blu (10). — [Lega Volley: A3 Girone Bianco](https://www.legavolley.it/2025/serie-a3-credem-banca-le-11-formazioni-del-girone-bianco/); [Formula Serie A3 2025-26](https://www.legavolley.it/formula-serie-a3-2025-26/)
- Serie A3 2026-27: the search summary reported 12 teams in Girone Bianco and 11 in Girone Blu, so about 23 teams. I did not read this on the primary page. — [Lega Volley: A3 al via con i Gironi Bianco e Blu (2026)](https://www.legavolley.it/2026/la-serie-a3-credem-banca-al-via-nel-weekend-con-i-gironi-bianco-e-blu/)
- Men's Serie B 2025-26: the official FIPAV "Organico Serie B Maschile" (17.06.2025) lists **111 named clubs plus 1 open slot (112 slots)**. — [FIPAV Organici BM-B1F-B2F 2025/26 (PDF)](https://guidapratica.federvolley.it/stagione-2025-2026/campionati-indoor/campionati/gp_campionati_organici_2526.pdf)

- Men's Serie B was played in 8 groups (A–H) of 13–14 teams. The group winners were promoted to A3 for 2026-27. — [FIPAV, Seconde Fasi Serie B Maschile 2025-26 (PDF)](https://www.federvolley.it/sites/default/files/news/allegati/Seconde%20Fasi%20Serie%20B%20Maschile%202025-26_0.pdf)

- Women's Serie B2 2025-26: **140 teams** in the FIPAV organico (numbered 1–140). — [FIPAV Organici 2025/26 (PDF)](https://guidapratica.federvolley.it/stagione-2025-2026/campionati-indoor/campionati/gp_campionati_organici_2526.pdf)

- Women's Serie A2 2025-26: **19 teams** in two groups (9 + 10). — [it.wikipedia Serie A2 2025-2026 (pallavolo femminile)](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serie_A2_2025-2026_(pallavolo_femminile))
- New women's **Serie A3 from 2026-27 with 16 teams**: 12 promoted from B1 plus 4 relegated from A2. B1 loses 12 teams to A3 and B2 sends 20 up to B1. Per the same article, men's Serie B becomes a single league ("girone unico" structure) from 2026-27. — [Volley News: Serie A3 femminile dal 2026/2027](https://www.volleynews.it/serie-a3-femminile-dal-2026-2027-cosa-cambia-per-i-campionati-di-b1-e-b2/)

### Inferences
- Italy team totals for 2025-26:
  - Men: 12 + 14 + 21 + 112 = **about 159**.
  - Women: 14 + 19 + 56 + 140 = **229**.
  - Total: **about 388 teams**.
- Italy for 2026-27: roughly the same total, plus about 16 for women's A3, plus about 2 for the larger men's A3. The tiers below are reshuffled.
- There are fewer distinct clubs than teams. Some clubs field both a men's and a women's team, and top clubs place youth or second teams in Serie B, B1 and B2 (Perugia, Trento, Imoco and Callipo appear in the B lists). A realistic count of **distinct paying entities is about 300–340**. This is an estimate, not counted.
- Professional tier (SuperLega, A1, A2 men and women): **59 teams**. Semi-pro tier (A3, B, B1, B2): **about 330 teams**.

### Gaps
- I did not find the exact number of women's B2 groups for 2025-26. 140 teams suggests 10 groups of 14, but that is inferred.
- I did not verify exact 2026-27 counts for Serie B men, B1 and B2 (the new structure).

## Other European countries: team counts per tier

### Takeaway
The top divisions have **12–16 teams per gender** in the major markets (Poland, Germany, France, Spain, Turkey). Second tiers range from one division of about 15 teams to multi-group leagues of 30+ teams (Spain's Superliga 2 men: 34 teams in 3 groups). Sourced data covers the big five well. The smaller countries (Belgium, the Netherlands, Greece, Czechia, Slovenia, Switzerland, Austria, Portugal, Romania, Serbia, Russia) were not sourced in this pass.

### Inferences

  - Germany: about 80 (15 + 12 + about 25 men's 2. BL + about 28–40 women's 2. BL). This fits the 95 licence applications.
  - Spain: at least 46 men's teams (12 + 34). Women not found.
  - Turkey: 14 men's top tier.
  - France: 13–14 women's top tier.
- **Unverified estimate (general knowledge, not sourced in this pass; verify before use):**
  - Turkey's Sultanlar Ligi has about 14–16 teams.
  - Turkey's men's and women's 1. Lig are multi-group leagues of about 20–30 teams each.
  - France's men's Ligue A has about 14 and Ligue B about 12–14.
  - France's "Élite" (the 3rd tier, federal) has about 24–32 teams per gender in groups.
  - Spain's Superliga Femenina has about 12–14 and Superliga Femenina 2 about 30+ in groups.
  - Each of the 11 smaller countries has about 8–12 top-tier teams per gender.
  - On these assumptions, the smaller countries add about 200–260 top-tier teams, and second tiers would roughly double that.
- Russia (Superleague men and women, about 14 and 12–14 teams, unverified) is in practice **not addressable**. Russian clubs have been excluded from CEV competitions since 2022, and sanctions complicate payment.
- A rough sourced-plus-estimated count for Europe excluding Italy: **about 700–1,000 teams** at the top-2 or top-3 tiers. The sourced core is about 200.

## The Americas: number of programs and teams

### Takeaway
The US college market is far larger than any pro league. It has about **348 NCAA D1 women's programs** and about 430 D3, plus D2, NAIA (about 215–220), NJCAA (about 310), and about 66 NCAA men's programs: roughly **1,600+ college programs** in total. US pro volleyball is small, with about 20–25 teams across MLV, LOVB Pro and Athletes Unlimited. Brazil's Superliga A has 24 teams (12 men, 12 women).

### Cited Findings
- **NCAA D1 women's**: 348 of 365 D1 institutions sponsor the sport as of the 2025 season (Wikipedia). ## Club budgets and technology or scouting spend

### Takeaway
Italian SuperLega clubs average about **€4.5M** a year (12 clubs, about €60M combined; top clubs above €7M). US NCAA D1 women's volleyball spans from about **$0.5M (median-type programs) to $7M (Nebraska)**. There are no reliable public figures for A2, A3, Serie B, PlusLiga, Bundesliga or Brazilian club budgets, and none for scouting or tech spend specifically.

### Cited Findings
- **SuperLega**: the 12 clubs' combined budget "sfiora i 60 milioni" a year. That is an average of about €4.5M per club, with top clubs above €7M. Source: Il Sole 24 Ore via Sport&Finanza, Oct 2024 (older data). — [Sport&Finanza](https://www.sportefinanza.it/2024/10/28/superlega-volley-budget/)
- **SuperLega**: clubs took about €18.6M a year from shirt sponsors in 2021-22, about €1.3M per club (dated). Lube, Perugia and Piacenza accounted for 46% of sponsor investment. — [Volley News](https://www.volleynews.it/le-societa-di-superlega-incassano-18-milioni-allanno-dagli-sponsor/); [Lega Volley / ChainOn study](https://www.legavolley.it/2022/chainon-on-lega-volley-season/?lang=en)
- **Italy, salaries**: a starting SuperLega player earns about €250k a year and a rotation player about €150k. The source is undated, so treat it with caution. — [Obiettivo Lavoro](https://obiettivolavoro.it/stipendi-pallavolo-serie-a-quanto-si-guadagna/)

### Inferences
- A €1–5k/season tool is about **0.02–0.1% of an average SuperLega budget**. Price is not the barrier at that level; the incumbent (DataVolley) and workflow lock-in are.
- Cost scales with tier. A2 registration fees (€3k) are 60% of SuperLega's, and A2 finances are described as fragile. **Unverified estimate:** A2 budgets are likely in the high hundreds of thousands to about €1.5M, A3 in the low hundreds of thousands, and Serie B, B1 and B2 in the tens of thousands to about €150k. At those levels, willingness to pay is likely in the low hundreds of euros a season per team. Verify with club interviews or press.
- NCAA D1 programs with $0.5M–$7M budgets resemble Italian A2-to-SuperLega spending power.

### Gaps
- Not found:
  - Credible 2025-26 budgets for Italian A2, A3 or Serie B clubs, PlusLiga, Bundesliga or Brazilian clubs (Polish-language searches returned only football).
  - Any published figure on scouting or technology spend per club.
  - A current NCAA D1 volleyball median expense (the NCAA Finances database or Knight Commission data would answer this).

## Staffing: scouts, coaching staff and youth sectors

### Takeaway
Pro teams typically run a **head coach plus 1–2 assistants, with a "scoutman" (statistician)**, a role sometimes filled by an assistant. US pro teams hire dedicated statisticians or technical coordinators. Italian clubs often run multiple teams (second teams in Serie B, B1 and B2, plus youth sectors). That supports **per-club rather than per-team pricing**.

### Cited Findings
- Professional teams tend to have "maybe three coaches – oftentimes it's just a head coach and an assistant", and you might also see a "Scoutman", i.e. a stats person, sometimes an assistant coach. This is an anecdotal coaching blog. — [Coaching Volleyball: Comparing NCAA and professional volleyball, Part II](https://coachingvb.com/comparing-ncaa-and-professional-volleyball-part-ii/)
- Major League Volleyball (DC) advertised a **Technical Coordinator** who acts as "primary statistician during all matches **using VolleyStation**" and produces scouting reports. — [TeamWork Online: MLV Technical Coordinator](https://www.teamworkonline.com/other-sports-jobs/mlv-jobs/mlv-dc-jobs/technical-coordinator-2180096)
- LOVB coaching staff include roles such as Director of Volleyball Operations bringing "data-driven strategy". — [LOVB PRO Coaches](https://www.lovb.com/lovb-coaches)
- The FIPAV runs formal statistician and match-analysis training ("Rilevazione Statistica e Match Analysis", 3rd-level coaching course). Scouting is part of the Italian coaching curriculum. — [FIPAV course material (PDF)](https://www.federvolley.it/sites/default/files/Settore%20Tecnico%20+%20Scuola%20+%20Antidoping/Rilevazione%20Statistica%20e%20Match%20%20%20%20Analysis%20-%20TERZO%20GRADO%202017%20%20-%20NO%20VALUTAZIONI.pdf)
- Top clubs appear multiple times in the FIPAV organici, which shows multi-team clubs:
  - Sir Safety Perugia, Trentino Volley, Callipo and Modena Volley Punto Zero in men's Serie B.
  - Imoco Volley and Chieri 76 in women's B1.
  - Sources: [FIPAV Organici 2025/26 (PDF)](https://guidapratica.federvolley.it/stagione-2025-2026/campionati-indoor/campionati/gp_campionati_organici_2526.pdf)
- FIPAV Serie B ripescaggi (readmission to the league) give priority to "Progetto Giovani" teams, i.e. youth-development rosters. — [FIPAV Organici 2025/26 (PDF)](https://guidapratica.federvolley.it/stagione-2025-2026/campionati-indoor/campionati/gp_campionati_organici_2526.pdf)

### Inferences
- Each pro or top semi-pro team likely has 2–4 staff users (head coach, assistant(s), scoutman or analyst). Club-level deals for Italian Serie B and up often cover several teams: a first team, a second team in B, B1 or B2, and the youth sector (U13–U19).
- Serie B-level teams likely lack a dedicated paid analyst. An assistant or volunteer does the stats, so ease of use and price matter more there than at the pro level.

### Gaps

## Scouting software penetration: DataVolley, Volleystation, Hudl

### Takeaway
DataVolley (Data Project, owned by Genius Sports since 2017) is the de facto standard for professional scouting. CEV required it for its matches (2015 guideline), and Data Project runs official stats for 65+ leagues and federations, including the RFEVB in Spain. Volleystation claims league partnerships with the Polish PLS (PlusLiga and Tauron Liga) and Volleyball World, and is used by US MLV. No source found gives a usage share by tier.

### Cited Findings
- DataVolley is "the standard software" used by technical staff to compile professional scouting files (.dvw). — [Springer, Journal of Big Data (2025): volleyball analytics on Italian women's league](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40537-025-01284-6)
- DataVolley is "the most widely used software for professional statistics analysis of volleyball games". — [Springer, Complex & Intelligent Systems (2022)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40747-022-00752-3)
- CEV guidelines (2015) make Data Project software use mandatory for CEV matches. — [CEV Data Project Software Guidelines (PDF)](https://inside.cev.eu/media/eulhmot0/dataproject-software-guidelines.pdf)
- Data Project says it serves 65+ leagues and federations, including the CEV, for official data collection. Genius Sports acquired Data Project in 2017. — [Data Project](https://www.dataproject.com/); [Data Project News](https://www.dataproject.com/it/News)
- Spain's RFEVB competitions (e.g., Superliga Masculina 2) are hosted on a Data Project competition site. — [rfevb-web.dataproject.com](https://rfevb-web.dataproject.com/CompetitionHome.aspx?ID=153)
- Lega Pallavolo Serie A publishes official, league-approved ("controllati e omologati") team stats for SuperLega 2025-26. I did not find which vendor's data feeds them. — [Lega Volley statistiche](https://www.legavolley.it/statistiche/?TipoStat=1.4)
- **Volleystation's claimed partnerships** come from company self-promotion (a LinkedIn post), not contracts:
  - The Polish PLS (PlusLiga and TauronLiga).
  - A renewed global partnership with Volleyball World (VNL, Beach Elite Series).
  - Source: [LinkedIn: Lukasz Wrobel, VolleyStation](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lukasz-wrobel-3a032987/)
- MLV team statisticians use VolleyStation. — [TeamWork Online MLV job](https://www.teamworkonline.com/other-sports-jobs/mlv-jobs/mlv-dc-jobs/technical-coordinator-2180096)
- No source found any formal Lega Pallavolo Serie A deal with Volleystation. Its known partnerships are Volleyball World (TV rights, 2021), StageUp (sponsor-value analytics from 2025-26) and a 2026 MoU with Japan's SV.League. — [Lega Volley: Volleyball World partnership](https://legavolley.it/2021/volleyball-world-launches-partnership-with-lega-pallavolo-serie-a/?lang=en); [Lega Volley: StageUp](https://www.legavolley.it/2026/lega-volley-and-stage-up-strongly-together/?lang=en); [SV.League press release](https://www.svleague.jp/en/v_men/topics/detail/24042)

### Inferences
- At SuperLega, A1, PlusLiga, Bundesliga and CEV level, near-universal scouting with DataVolley or Volleystation should be assumed. A new product would compete as a complement (player development, training plans, calendars, lightweight live stats), not as a replacement for scouting.
- The semi-pro tiers (A3, Serie B, B1, B2, 2. Bundesliga, Superliga 2) are less saturated and more price-sensitive. This is unverified and there is no usage data.
- Hudl: no volleyball-specific penetration data was found. Hudl is strong in US colleges and high schools (general knowledge, unverified here).

### Gaps
- Not found: usage share by tier, whether Serie A rules mandate scouting files, or which vendor supplies Lega Volley's official stats.
