## 1. Which products semi-pro/pro and NCAA clubs actually use

### Takeaway
At pro/semi-pro level in Europe the standard is Data Project's DataVolley (DV4, .dvw files), with VolleyStation the main modern challenger (strong in Poland, Volleyball World events and increasingly US college/pro). In NCAA, Hudl's VolleyMetrics is the video-exchange and analyst-coded stats backbone; Hudl/Balltime (AI) and cheap tap apps (SoloStats, iStatVball) dominate youth/HS.

### Cited Findings
- Data Project claims ~98% of men's and women's national teams used its software at the 2018 FIVB World Championship and 2019 World Cup, 65+ leagues/federations (incl. CEV) rely on it for data collection, 10,000+ daily users; partner logos include CEV, Lega Volley (men + women), LNV (France), TVF (Turkey), Nevobo (NL), Poland (vendor self-claims) — [Data Project volleyball page](https://www.dataproject.com/EU/en/Volleyball)
- Academic paper on the Italian women's league: DataVolley is "the standard software to help the technical staff to compile professional volleyball scouting files (in .dvw format)"; dataset from Trentino Volley staff, Serie A2 women 2023-25. It also notes DataVolley is closed-source, so users cannot customise features or integrate new models — [Springer, Journal of Big Data 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40537-025-01284-6)
- DV4 can push live data to the FIVB database (Live Match / VIS procedure) — [Data Project FIVB Match online procedure PDF](https://dataprojectwebsoftware.blob.core.windows.net/servizi/FIVB/FIVB_Match_online_procedure.pdf)
- Competitor guide: DataVolley "is the absolute standard in the elite (Olympic Games, Italian SuperLega, Polish PlusLiga)" but overkill for "95% of clubs" (marketing piece by a competitor) — [Tacticode guide 2026](https://tacticode.app/en/blog/volleyball-video-analysis-software-guide/)
- VolleyStation (2021 self-claim): all PlusLiga clubs use its software and league statistics are "powered by VS" — [VolleyStation LinkedIn article](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/2021-facts-you-may-know-volleystation-volleystation)
- VolleyStation exec profile (2026): serves PLS competitions (PlusLiga, TauronLiga), renewed global partnership with Volleyball World (VNL etc.), official data management partner of Pro Volleyball Federation — [LinkedIn, Lukasz Wrobel](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lukasz-wrobel-3a032987/) (self-published, unverified)
- Polish press (26 Feb 2026): after Stanford became a client, VolleyStation signed ~100 US university agreements within 1.5-2 months; clients include US men's and women's national teams, Polish clubs/national teams; deals with PLS and PZPS; via Sportradar, clubs and whole leagues in Austria, Brazil, Turkey; official partner of LOVB, works with MLV clubs; provided stats services for Paris Olympics; AI analysis from a phone behind the court in 30-40 min — [xyz.pl, 26 Feb 2026](https://xyz.pl/sztuczna-inteligencja-w-siatkowce-bank-rozbija-polska-firma-volleystation/)
- University of Wisconsin video coordinator uses Volleymetrics (video exchange) and VolleyStation for analytics — [Big Ten Central](https://big10central.com/school/wisconsin/ncaa-volleyball-technology-tv-scouting/article_7f49a036-1f6e-596c-b013-6c237081f20d.html); Minnesota mines data from volleymetrics.com — [Star Tribune](https://m.startribune.com/ncaa-bound-gophers-volleyball-team-driven-by-data/501500462/)
- Hudl acquired VolleyMetrics (announced 13 Dec 2017); VolleyMetrics founded 2013, serving 350+ collegiate and professional organisations at that time — [Hudl blog](https://hudl.com/blog/hudl-acquires-volleymetrics-strengthens-solutions-for-volleyball-at-every-level)
- VolleyMetrics functions moving to the Hudl platform from mid-August 2026; match video still uploaded to Volleymetrics for international video exchange compliance; Hudl's volleyball page offers college breakdowns "through Volleymetrics or Hudl Assist" — [smashvision cost guide](https://smashvision.ai/blog/how-much-does-volleyball-stats-software-cost), [Hudl volleyball](https://www.hudl.com/sports/volleyball)
- Hudl acquired Balltime (AI volleyball, founded 2022 by Tom Raz and Dan Banon), announced Feb 2025; claims range from 3,800+ to 12,000+ teams — [Hudl blog](https://www.hudl.com/blog/hudl-expands-volleyball-focus-through-game-changing-acquisition-of-balltime), [Silicon Prairie News, Jun 2025](https://siliconprairienews.com/2025/06/lincoln-based-hudl-acquires-titan-sports-and-balltime-expanding-access-to-performance-data-for-athletes/)
- Hudl acquired Krossover (one of its largest competitors) in May 2019; Krossover accounts/breakdowns supported until summer 2020 — so Krossover is no longer an independent competitor — [Hudl Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudl), [Hudl blog](https://www.hudl.com/blog/krossover-joins-hudl)
- England senior men's national team analyst chose Hudl (players have access via a Super League deal with Volleyball England) over VolleyMetrics; would pick VolleyMetrics if they had no DataVolley — [thevolleyballanalyst, Jan 2024](https://thevolleyballanalyst.wordpress.com/2024/01/21/hudl-vs-volleymetrics/)
- SoloStats 123 is made by Rotate123 and listed as "trusted by the AVCA" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/solostats-123-volleyball-stats/id499803638)

### Inferences
- The realistic "incumbent stack" of a Serie B-to-SuperLega club is DataVolley 4 (Lite or Pro) on a Windows laptop run by one scout, plus a video tool; above A2 there is usually a dedicated analyst. A new web app competes there less on depth than on collaboration (cloud sharing, coaches/players accessing reports without the analyst) and on .dvw compatibility.
- Hudl is consolidating US volleyball (VolleyMetrics 2017, Krossover 2019, Balltime 2025). In NCAA, video exchange via VolleyMetrics is a network effect a newcomer cannot replace; the open flank is a cheaper or faster live-scouting and reporting layer, with export to VolleyMetrics/DV formats.
- VolleyStation is the fastest-growing challenger and is going both pro-Europe and US (Stanford, LOVB, PVF, MLV), including AI video. It is the most direct threat for a new entrant.

### Gaps
- No independent, verified usage share per league (e.g. % of SuperLega/A2/Bundesliga/LNV/Superliga Spain clubs on DV vs VS). Partner logos do not say which product each league uses.
- No sources found for Brazilian Superliga-specific tools, Spain/Turkey local tools, or Vbstats / StatEasy / "Volleyball Statistics (TeamStats)". "VolleyStats" as a named app was not found (only "Volleyball Stat!" by Geoffrey Tywyll, a free iPad app — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/volleyball-stat/id459540544)).
- Veo/Pixellot volleyball adoption and pricing: no source found in this pass.

## 2. Prices: exact or approximate, public vs quote

Everything aimed at institutions (VolleyStation, VolleyMetrics, Hudl Assist, Balltime Team, Hudl college) is quote-only.

### Inferences
- Price anchors for a per-season web app aimed at Serie B to A1: DataVolley Lite €299/yr and Pro €799/yr per licence (one scout seat; extra devices via Reader/Web Client) is the reference a European club already pays. Clubs above A2 often pay for several licences plus video hardware and staff, so the software line itself is small compared with analyst salaries.
- AI video is priced far higher (SmashVision €3,000-10,000/season; VolleyStation AI quoted). A product offering live tap scouting + AI text scouting + reports + awards could sit between €300 and €1,500/season per team, undercutting AI video and matching or slightly beating DV Pro on collaboration (an inference, not a sourced benchmark).
- US college pricing is opaque; the only number found ($500-2,000/team/yr for VolleyMetrics) is a competitor estimate.

### Gaps
- No public VolleyStation price (software, streaming, AI). No forum/Reddit-reported quotes found in this pass.

- DataVolley hardware (dongle, video capture card) costs and multi-licence discounts not found.
- No Reddit r/volleyball / r/volleyballcoach quotes found; searches returned vendor content.

## 3. Features and segment each product dominates

### Takeaway
DataVolley = deepest analysis, Windows desktop, offline, code-entry; VolleyStation = similar depth with more modern UX, league data feeds and AI; VolleyMetrics = outsourced coding + exchange; Balltime/Hudl Assist/SmashVision/SportsVisio = AI from video, no coder; SoloStats/iStatVball/Ace = cheap tap stats for HS/youth.

### Cited Findings
- DV4 Lite vs Pro features: Lite has video import/sync, offline and online calendar, scouting both teams, player/skill/rotation/zone/direction analysis, setter distribution, default worksheets; Pro adds video capture and streaming, dv2-vis export (FIVB), extended/custom codes, advanced filters, consecutive distribution, custom worksheet formulas, video montage, Web Client App/Client/Reader (up to 4 devices per key) — [Data Project DV4](https://www.dataproject.com/Products/EN/en/Volleyball/DataVolley4)
- Data Project also sells Web Competition Manager (federations/leagues), VolleyBoard (in-venue scoreboard and stats), e-Scoresheet, Video Sharing 4, VideoCheck (challenge system) — [Data Project volleyball](https://www.dataproject.com/EU/en/Volleyball)
- Free Data Volley 4 Player app to analyse matches imported from DV4 Pro — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/data-volley-4-player/id1277570273)
- VolleyMetrics: very detailed stats (4-point pass grading, missed digs coded, attack combinations, graded technique), scoresheet upload for names/lineups, in-depth PDF reports; stats not hyperlinked to video, video must be compressed under 3 GB. Hudl: stats linked to clips but limited ("NCAA-style") stats, tagging errors (libero credited with kills), downballs counted as attacks — [thevolleyballanalyst](https://thevolleyballanalyst.wordpress.com/2024/01/21/hudl-vs-volleymetrics/)
- VolleyMetrics tracks touches within 12 hours; Axis IP cameras approved for auto-recording — [smashvision](https://smashvision.ai/blog/how-much-does-volleyball-stats-software-cost)
- Balltime features: downtime removal, auto filters, vertical highlight reels, serve/attack/set speed, jump detection, recruiting video exports/templates — [Hudl Balltime pricing](https://www.hudl.com/pricing/balltime)
- VolleyStation AI measures jump height, serve speed, distance from optimal positioning, setter-to-attacker time — [xyz.pl](https://xyz.pl/sztuczna-inteligencja-w-siatkowce-bank-rozbija-polska-firma-volleystation/)
- SportsVisio: AI stats and highlights, post-game "often within 24 hours", school/club/youth; Hudl Assist HS volleyball breakdowns "coming in 2026"; DataVolley/DataVideo for varsity and higher — [SportsVisio, Jan 2026](https://www.sportsvisio.com/stories/volleyball-ai-analysis-platforms-comparisons)
- Click&Scout: bench tablet scouting, per-rotation reports, beach volley and Green Volley support, DV-compatible files — [Click&Scout](https://www.dataproject.com/Products/EU/en/Pallavolo/ClickAndScout)

### Inferences
- No incumbent clearly combines (a) offline-first live tap scouting in a browser/PWA, (b) AI/text-assisted scouting, (c) cloud reports shared with players, and (d) shareable social awards cards. DV is offline but desktop and siloed; Hudl/Balltime are cloud but video-first and US-centric; VolleyStation is closest to a full stack.
- Shareable awards/"Wrapped" cards appear in none of the sources, so they are a possible differentiator for club marketing and fan engagement.

### Gaps
- VolleyStation feature/platform details beyond press/LinkedIn (the volleystation.com fetch returned no content).
- Offline behaviour of VolleyStation, Hudl, Balltime not documented in sources.

## 4. Known weaknesses / complaints (positioning)

### Takeaway
The recurring complaints about the incumbents are DataVolley's Windows-only, dongle-based, code-heavy, single-laptop workflow and steep learning curve; Hudl's shallow stats and tagging errors; VolleyMetrics' slow turnaround and clunky uploads; opaque pricing across institutional tools.

### Cited Findings
- DV4 requirements: Windows 8.1+, USB Type-A port for dongle, internet for licence activation, not compatible with ARM processors (so no Apple Silicon Macs or ARM Windows tablets) — [Data Project DV4](https://www.dataproject.com/Products/EN/en/Volleyball/DataVolley4)
- Click&Scout "no longer works on macOS and can't be bought for it" — [Click&Scout](https://www.dataproject.com/Products/EU/en/Pallavolo/ClickAndScout)
- DataVolley data "sits on the analyst's laptop", so coaches/players must go through the analyst for reports and clips; the author's ideal is "DataVolley available online" — [thevolleyballanalyst](https://thevolleyballanalyst.wordpress.com/2024/01/21/hudl-vs-volleymetrics/)
- DataVolley coding requires typing alphanumeric codes fast and takes months to master; "overbuilt and expensive" for most clubs (competitor marketing) — [Tacticode](https://tacticode.app/en/blog/volleyball-video-analysis-software-guide/)
- DataVolley/DataVideo weaknesses: "time cost, staffing requirement, steeper learning curve" — [SportsVisio](https://www.sportsvisio.com/stories/volleyball-ai-analysis-platforms-comparisons)
- DataVolley is closed-source, no custom models or integration — [Springer 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40537-025-01284-6)
- Hudl ecosystem "can become complex across products, availability differs by level" — [SportsVisio](https://www.sportsvisio.com/stories/volleyball-ai-analysis-platforms-comparisons)
- Hudl stat limitations and tagging errors; VolleyMetrics stats not linked to video, 3 GB compression chore — [thevolleyballanalyst](https://thevolleyballanalyst.wordpress.com/2024/01/21/hudl-vs-volleymetrics/)
- VolleyStation Score app rated 2.6/5 (40 ratings) on App Store — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/1614399577)
- Subscription fees "a significant expense" for small clubs (VolleyStation) — [SaaSHub](https://www.saashub.com/compare-volleystation-vs-pro-analyst)
- Volleyball Ace Stats last iOS update Oct 2023 (stale) — [smashvision](https://smashvision.ai/blog/how-much-does-volleyball-stats-software-cost)

### Inferences
- Positioning: "DataVolley-grade live scouting that runs on any tablet or phone in a browser, works offline, and puts reports in coaches' and players' hands the moment the match ends", with .dvw import/export to reduce switching cost for the incumbent analyst.
- Transparent per-season public pricing is itself a differentiator against quote-only institutional vendors.

### Gaps
- Few independent user reviews; most critique comes from competitor blogs (SmashVision, Tacticode, SportsVisio, Varsity Score) and one analyst blog. No Reddit threads located.

## 5. Market dominance indicators

### Takeaway
DataVolley: FIVB/CEV/national-team ubiquity and Italian league partnership. VolleyStation: PLS/PZPS, Volleyball World, LOVB, PVF, Sportradar distribution, fast US college uptake. Hudl: owns VolleyMetrics, Krossover, Balltime, so it dominates US.

### Cited Findings
- Data Project: ~98% of national teams at 2018 WCh/2019 World Cup; 65+ leagues/federations incl. CEV; 10,000+ daily users; partners Lega Volley, LNV, TVF, Nevobo — [Data Project](https://www.dataproject.com/EU/en/Volleyball) (self-claim)
- Data Project founded by Emilio Spirito; software used by FIVB at the Olympics — [Panorama](https://www.panorama.it/tempo-libero/tecnologia/l-italiano-che-ha-vinto-le-olimpiadi-con-un-software-per-la-pallavolo)
- VolleyStation: PLS (PlusLiga, TauronLiga), Volleyball World renewal, PVF official data partner — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lukasz-wrobel-3a032987/); ~100 US universities after Stanford, US national teams, LOVB official partner, MLV clubs, Sportradar channel into Austria/Brazil/Turkey, Paris Olympics stats — [xyz.pl Feb 2026](https://xyz.pl/sztuczna-inteligencja-w-siatkowce-bank-rozbija-polska-firma-volleystation/)
- VolleyStation is not listed among CEV official partners — search result summary of [CEV homepage](https://www.cev.eu/)
- Hudl: VolleyMetrics (2017, 350+ orgs), Krossover (May 2019), Balltime (Feb 2025) — sources in section 1

### Inferences
- Europe: two-player market at the top (Data Project incumbent, VolleyStation challenger), both tied to federations/leagues through data feeds, which a club-level app cannot easily dislodge. A newcomer should integrate (import/export .dvw) rather than replace league feeds.
- Americas: Hudl is the platform default; VolleyStation is the alternative for analytics-heavy programs and the new pro leagues.

### Gaps
- Funding amounts for VolleyStation and Balltime pre-acquisition not found; Balltime acquisition price undisclosed in sources.
- No verified data on which LNV/Superliga Spain/Bundesliga/Brazilian Superliga clubs use which tool.
