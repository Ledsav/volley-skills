## Our two roles

We have two different roles under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), depending on the data:

- **Club data: we are a processor.** Each club decides what it records about its players, guardians and staff, and why. For that data, the club is the **controller** and we are its **processor**: we host and process it only on the club's instructions, under a data processing agreement with the club.
- **Our own data: we are the controller.** For the data we need to run the service and the contract with each club, listed below, we are the **controller**.

## Club data (we are the processor)

Club data includes players' details (such as name, date of birth, jersey number and position), guardians' contact details, skill ratings, development plans, physical tests, match statistics, trainings and calendars.

**Your club is responsible for the player data it enters. Please contact your club** to ask what it holds about you or your child, to correct or delete it, or for any other request. The club has the tools in the service to answer these requests.

If you write to us about club data, we pass your request to the club. If you do not know how to reach the club, ask us and we will tell you how to contact it.

What we do with club data, and the security measures we apply, are set out in the Data Processing Agreement at /legal/dpa.

## Who receives the data

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

The service runs on Google Firebase and Google Cloud. The sub-processors we use, what they process and where are listed at /legal/subprocessors.

Inside a club, club data is visible to the club's own members, according to the access the club gives them. We have no access to club data inside the service; our access at the infrastructure level is limited as set out in the Data Processing Agreement.

## Transfers outside the European Union

Club data is stored in the European Union. Some Google services the service relies on (sign-in, delivery of the application and abuse protection) may process data in the United States. These transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and on the standard contractual clauses in Google's data processing terms.

## AI features

AI features are off by default, and only a club admin can switch them on for the club. Staff then choose an AI provider and use their own key with it. That provider works for the club, as the club's own processor, not for us.

In text scouting, the service replaces the club's player names with jersey numbers before sending text to the AI provider. AI drafting of exercises and trainings sends only the coach's request and the text of exercises in the club's library, with no player data from the service. Staff should not type personal data into AI requests. AI is never used to rate, rank or select players.

## Photos in award cards

Photos added to award cards are processed on the device only, to create the image. They are never uploaded or stored by the service. A photo of a minor can be used only once the club has recorded a guardian's consent in the service. For photos of adults, the club relies on its own lawful basis.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format, within the limits the GDPR sets. For data we hold on the basis of our legitimate interest, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your situation.

For club data, please contact your club first (see above). For data we control, write to {{OPERATOR_PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority. While we are established in Luxembourg, our lead supervisory authority is the Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD), Luxembourg. You may also complain to the authority of the EU country where you live, work, or where you think the problem happened.

## No tracking or analytics

The service uses no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

## Storage on your device

The service stores on your device only what it needs to work and the preferences you set yourself. It uses no cookies of its own and nothing for tracking, so it shows no cookie banner. Google's reCAPTCHA, used by App Check, may store its own data in your browser to protect the service from abuse.

The browser's IndexedDB holds your sign-in session (Firebase Authentication), an offline copy of the club data you use, so that matches can be scouted without a signal, and small technical records the Firebase software keeps for itself, such as the App Check token. Signing out clears the offline copy (if another open tab of the service still uses it, it is cleared at a later sign-out).

The service also uses these keys in the browser's localStorage, or sessionStorage where stated:
