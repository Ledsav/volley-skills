# Sub-processors

> **Draft — under legal review.**

Version 2026-10-22.

This is the list of sub-processors that {{OPERATOR_NAME}} (the "operator") engages to provide Volley Skills (the "service") to clubs. It is Annex III of the Data Processing Agreement (the "DPA"). It is versioned separately from the DPA: changes are announced 30 days in advance, and a club may object as set out in D5 of the DPA.

## Current sub-processors

| Sub-processor | Service | Personal data | Location and transfer basis |

| Google Ireland Limited (Google Cloud / Firebase) | Cloud Firestore: the service's database | All club data | European Union (EU multi-region location eur3) |
| Google Ireland Limited (Google Cloud / Firebase) | Firebase Hosting: delivery of the service's web application files | Request data, such as IP address and browser details | Google's global network, which may include the United States: EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses |

Google's own sub-processors, certifications and data processing terms are published by Google.

## Not sub-processors

The following are not sub-processors of the operator:

- **AI providers chosen by the club.** AI features are off by default, and a club admin decides whether to switch them on. Club staff then choose an AI provider and use their own key with it. Such an AI provider is the club's own processor, engaged by the club under its own agreement with the provider. It is not engaged by the operator, so it is not on this list. The provider presets in the service, including any "recommended" label, are convenience defaults, not an engagement of the provider by the operator.
- **A payment provider, when payments are introduced.** It will process billing data as part of the operator's own processing as a controller, described in the privacy notice, not as a sub-processor for club data.

## Changes to this list

Before adding or replacing a sub-processor, the operator publishes a new version of this list and gives clubs 30 days' notice, by a banner in the service shown to club admins and by an email to each club's owner email. A club may object within 15 days of the notice, as set out in D5 of the DPA.
